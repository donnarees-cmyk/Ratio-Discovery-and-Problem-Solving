📘 Mrs. Francis' Ratio Toolkit & Reference Guide

1. Ratio Notations

Ratios compare two quantities. Write them as 3 to 4, 3 : 4, or 3/4.

2. Part vs. Whole

Part-to-Part: Compares one group to another.
Part-to-Whole: Compares one group to total items.

3. Scaling Equivalent Ratios

Multiply or divide both terms in a ratio by the same non-zero number to create equivalent ratios.
